FaceMorph Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 5, 2026

What FaceMorph does

FaceMorph helps you capture one aligned face photo per day and turn your photo history into a timelapse. This policy explains what data the app uses and how you control it.

Data we collect

  • Google account information such as your name, email address, and profile image, to sign you in and create your FaceMorph profile.
  • Photos that you capture or import, together with their capture dates, to synchronize your timeline and provide optional feed features.
  • Settings such as visibility and reminder preference, to provide the features you choose.
  • Purchase information supplied by Google Play when you buy FaceMorph Premium. FaceMorph does not receive your payment card details.

How your data is used

  • Face alignment is processed on your device using Google ML Kit. The camera image is uploaded only after you choose to save it.
  • Your profile is stored with Firebase, and your photos are stored with Bunny.net to keep your timeline available across sessions.
  • New photos are private by default. If you enable public visibility, your photos and basic profile information can appear in the FaceMorph public feed. You can turn this off in Settings.
  • Reminder notifications are scheduled locally on your device. We do not use them to track you.

Sharing and retention

We do not sell your personal data. Public feed content is shared with other FaceMorph users only when you enable public visibility. We keep account data and photos while your account is active, unless a legal obligation requires a longer retention period.

Your choices

You can change visibility and reminder settings in the app. You can request deletion from Settings or use the external account deletion page. Account deletion removes your authentication account, profile, photos, and related FaceMorph data.
